About UptoZishu

UptoZishu is a simple, clean app store for Android, made for independent developers and the people who like to try their apps.

Find apps you will actually use
• Browse apps by category and see what is new on the home screen
• Search with filters, and quickly come back to your recent searches
• Read screenshots, ratings and reviews before you install
• Mark reviews as helpful, and sort them to see the best ones first

Know what you are installing
• Every app is reviewed by our team before it goes live
• Developers can get a Verified badge
• Open an app's information page to see its developer, permissions and security details in one place

Stay up to date
• Get a notification when an app you installed has a new version
• Update all your apps with one tap
• UptoZishu can update itself too, right from the app

Coming soon
• See upcoming apps in the Coming soon section
• Pre-register for an app and get a notification in the app when it is released

Download and install
• Downloads can be paused and resumed, so a weak connection does not make you start over
• Your library keeps track of the apps you installed

Are you a developer?
Publish your app for free from the Developer Console. Share a pre-release page, release when you are ready, and reach people who want to discover new apps.

Please note: Android asks you to confirm every app installation, and UptoZishu cannot skip that step. Some apps in the store may show Sponsored or Featured labels, because developers can choose to promote their apps.
