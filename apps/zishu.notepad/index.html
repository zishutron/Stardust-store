About Zishu Notepad

Zishu Notepad is a clean and modern cloud-based notes app designed to make writing, organizing, and accessing your personal notes simple.

Create notes instantly, keep your ideas organized, and access them across devices with your account. Your notes are stored securely in the cloud, so you can continue where you left off instead of relying on a single device.

Key Features

- Create and manage personal notes
- Secure email and password authentication
- Cloud-based note storage
- Access your notes across devices
- Automatic saving while editing
- Search through your notes
- Favorite important notes
- Recently deleted notes with restore support
- Clean, distraction-free writing experience
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop
- Simple, professional interface

Zishu Notepad is built for everyday writing — from quick ideas and personal notes to important information you want available whenever you need it.
