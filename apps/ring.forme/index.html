About Ring For Me · Love Alarm

Have you ever walked past someone in a crowded cafe, street, or college campus and thought to yourself— *"I wish I could talk to them"*?

Ring For Me turns that exact feeling into reality!

Inspired by the iconic concept of the K-Drama 'Love Alarm', Ring For Me transforms your surroundings into a beautiful, magical social experience. It is not just another networking app—it is a real-time bridge connecting hearts nearby.

---

Key Features:

Live Proximity Radar:

See who is nearby within your radius (10m, 20m, or 50m) in real-time. Never miss a chance connection!

🔔 Instant Ring Notifications:

Whenever someone matching your vibes, interests, or preferences enters your radius, your phone will ring to let you know.

💞 Mutual Rings:

If you ring someone and they respond back, congratulations! It’s a **Mutual Ring**—time to start a conversation without any awkwardness.

Smart Ice-Breakers:

Never worry about how to start a conversation. The app suggests personalized ice-breakers based on shared hobbies and vibe tags.

Ghost Mode & Full Privacy:

Your privacy is our top priority. When you want quiet time, simply turn on **Ghost Mode** with a single tap to disappear from the radar.

🌍 Multi-Language Support:

Language should never be a barrier to love or friendship! **Ring For Me** fully supports **13+ languages**—including **English, Hindi, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, and more**—so you can connect comfortably in your preferred language.

---

💡 How It Works:

1. Create your profile and select your vibe tags (e.g., Romantic, Chill, Adventurous).
2. Turn on the Live Radar whenever you are out and about.
3. Get notified with a ring as soon as a matching profile gets close!
4. Send a ring, get a mutual match, and start your story.

---

Whether you are looking for a soulmate or simply want to meet like-minded friends around you—Ring For Me makes every encounter unforgettable.

Download today and let love ring in your world! ✦
