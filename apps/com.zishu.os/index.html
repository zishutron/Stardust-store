About Zishu OS

Zishu OS – Your Phone. Your Style.

Zishu OS is a complete home experience for Android, built by ZISHU TRON. It replaces your launcher with a clean, fluid and fully customizable home screen, and brings its own Phone, Photos, Messages and Browser apps along with it. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking. Your layout, your look and your sounds stay on your phone.

HOME SCREEN
• Free layout with pages, folders, widgets and a Today page
• Choose icon shape, icon pack style, size and spacing
• Wallpapers, blur and a smooth app drawer with alphabet index
• Gesture control: double tap, swipe and navigation gestures, each one assignable
• Back up and restore your whole layout

CONTROL CENTER
• Swipe down from the status bar for quick toggles, brightness, volume and a music player
• Set screen timeout, open the calculator, flashlight and more
• Works over other apps, with its own settings

NOTIFICATION CENTER
• Glass-style notification list on the home screen and the lock screen
• Music and video controls with previous, play or pause, next and a seek slider
• Live download progress with a cancel button
• End a call straight from its notification
• Switch every feature on or off in Control Panel

RECENT APPS
• A card-style recent apps view with swipe-to-close and Close All
• Choose how far back to list apps and the card size

LOCK SCREEN AND CHARGING
• A custom lock screen with clock, widgets and notifications
• Unlock with your phone's own PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint
• A charging animation you can customize

ZISHU PHONE
• Dialer, contacts, favorites and call history
• A polished in-call screen with notification controls

ZISHU PHOTOS
• Library by years, months, days or all photos, plus Collections
• A smooth viewer: pinch to zoom, pan, two-finger rotate that is saved into the file, hold for 2x speed, and Picture-in-Picture for videos
• Favorites, Hidden and Recently Deleted albums protected by your phone's screen lock
• Editing tools, duplicate, add to album and change date or location
• Choose your accent color and light or dark appearance
• Photos never connects to the internet

ZISHU MESSAGES
• SMS and MMS with threads, reactions and pinned or hidden chats
• Customize each conversation's look
• Share text or pictures to Zishu Messages from other apps

ZISHU BROWSER
• A fast, clean and private browser with tabs, bookmarks, reading tools and downloads
• Start Page, tab overview and a native interface
• The only part of Zishu OS that uses the internet

SOUNDS
• Short sounds for gestures, panels and notifications
• One master switch, a separate setting for each place, or one sound for everything

PRIVACY FIRST
• No ads, no analytics, no telemetry
• The launcher, Photos, Messages and Phone never connect to the internet
• Permissions are requested only when you use the feature that needs them, and each one is explained inside the app

PERMISSIONS AND WHY
Zishu OS asks for special access only for features you turn on: Notification access (Notification Center and music controls), Accessibility service (gestures and Control Center over apps), Display over other apps, Usage access (recent apps), Device admin (turn the screen off), Photos and videos, Contacts, Phone, SMS, Notifications and foreground services. Every permission is optional, and the rest of Zishu OS keeps working without it. A full list is in Settings, inside the app.

SET UP IN A MINUTE
Open Zishu OS, follow the short onboarding, set it as your home app, and make it yours in Control Panel.
