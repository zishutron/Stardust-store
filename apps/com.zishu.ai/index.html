About Zishu AI: Voice Assistant

Zishu — Your Personal AI Voice Assistant

Talk naturally. Ask anything. Get things done — with just your voice.

Zishu is a voice-first AI assistant built for real conversations. Speak in Hindi, English, or Hinglish and Zishu understands you the way a human would. Whether you want to open an app, send a message, search the web, set a reminder, or just ask a question — Zishu listens, understands, and responds with a natural voice.

No typing. No menus. No complicated commands. Just talk.

🎙️ TALK NATURALLY — IN YOUR LANGUAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Speak in Hindi, English, or Hinglish. Zishu understands all three and replies in a natural, human-like voice. Ask anything — from "Vasco da Gama India kab aaya tha?" to "What's the weather today?" — and get real answers, not scripted replies.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Just say what you need. Zishu can:

• Open any installed app — WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, Chrome, Gmail, Spotify, and more
• Call your contacts by name — "Rahul ko call karo"
• Send SMS to contacts — "Mummy ko message bhejo"
• Search the web — "Google par search karo..."
• Open Android settings — WiFi, Bluetooth, display, sound, battery
• Open Google Maps and find places
• Toggle flashlight on or off
• Set alarms and reminders — "7 baje alarm lagao"
• Save quick notes — "Note save karo shopping list"
• Check battery, network, and device info
• Get weather, time, and date
• Translate between languages
• Do calculations — "125 × 48 kya hai?"

Everything through your voice, in seconds.
