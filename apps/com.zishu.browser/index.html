About Zishu Browser

Zishu Browser is a fast, lightweight and privacy-focused web browser for Android. It's built for real browsing — with a clean interface, modern features, and complete user control. And at just 242 KB, it installs quickly and runs smoothly even on low-end devices.

KEY FEATURES

MULTI-TAB BROWSING
Open as many tabs as you need. Switch, duplicate and close tabs with a tap. A clean tab overview keeps everything organised.

PRIVATE BROWSING
Open private tabs that don't save history, cookies or form data between sessions. Private tabs close cleanly when you're done.

SMART ADDRESS BAR
One field for everything — type a URL or a search query. Zishu detects which one you mean and takes you straight there.

BOOKMARKS & HISTORY
Save pages you care about and find past visits quickly. Everything is stored locally on your device and easy to search, edit or clear.

BUILT-IN DOWNLOAD MANAGER
Track downloads with live progress, speed and remaining time. Cancel, retry or open finished files in a single tap.

DESKTOP SITE MODE
Request the full desktop version of any website whenever you want a wider layout on a smaller screen.

SITE PERMISSIONS
Control camera, microphone, location and notification access per site. Nothing is ever granted silently.

FIND IN PAGE
Search for text inside any page and jump between matches with next and previous navigation.

COMPLETE SETTINGS PANEL
Choose your search engine (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo), set your homepage, switch between light and dark themes, adjust zoom, control JavaScript, popups, autoplay and cookies — all in one place.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PRIVACY & CONTROL

Zishu Browser gives you practical controls, not marketing promises:

• Private browsing mode
• Cookie controls — allow or block, clear any time
• Do Not Track signal
• HTTPS preference with certificate warnings
• Per-site permission management
• One-tap clearing of history, cookies, cache and site data

Zishu Browser does not collect, transmit or sell your personal browsing data. Browsing history, bookmarks, downloads and settings are stored locally on your device. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers embedded in the app.
