Privacy policy updated 13 November 2026

UptoZishu is a web app store. You can browse and download apps without an account. An account is only needed to post reviews.

What we store on our servers

DataWhyPublic?
Name, username, bio, sign-up dateYour profile and reviewsYes, on your public profile
Email and password (or Google sign-in)Signing you inNo, never shown
Reviews (rating, text, your name and username)Show reviews on the app pageYes
Reports you send about an appReview and remove bad appsNo
App counters: views, downloads (also per day), ratingShow popularity to everyone and to the app's developerYes, as totals, not linked to you

If you only browse, no personal data about you is saved on our servers.

What stays on your phone

  • Saved apps and download history: one list on this phone, the same with or without login. It is never uploaded.
  • Your theme (light or dark).
  • Small temporary items so your latest review or download shows before the store page refreshes.

Clearing your browser data removes these and we cannot restore them.

How we use it

  • To run your account, profile and reviews.
  • To keep one review per person per app and usernames unique.
  • To count views and downloads.
  • To handle reports and keep the store safe.

We do not sell or share your personal data with anyone. It stays in our Firebase database, which we use only to run the store.

Services we use

  • Firebase (Google) for sign-in (email or Google) and our database.
  • GitHub Pages to host the store's pages.
  • Developer download links: when you tap Download you go to a link chosen by the app's developer, and their own privacy policy applies there.

These services may process basic technical data such as your IP address under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and bio in Library, Settings, Edit profile. Your username is locked once chosen.
  • Delete your account in Library, Settings, Delete account. This removes your profile, username and all your reviews, and updates app ratings.
  • Report an app in Library, Settings, Report an app.
  • Ask us about your data by email.

Children

UptoZishu is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Continuing to use the store means you accept the update.
