Privacy policy updated 18 November 2026

UptoZishu is a web app store run by ZISHU TRON. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and the choices you have. By using the store you agree to it. You can browse and download apps without an account. An account is needed only to post reviews, send feedback, send reports and use support.

If you only browse, no personal data about you is saved on our servers.

What stays on your phone

  • Saved apps and download history: one list on this phone, the same with or without login. It is never uploaded.
  • Your theme (light or dark).
  • Small temporary items so your latest review or download shows before the store page refreshes.

Clearing your browser data removes these and we cannot restore them.

What other people can see

  • Everyone: your public profile and your reviews.
  • App developers: feedback sent about their apps, and the reviews and ratings on their apps. They do not get your email address from us.
  • Our team: feedback, reports, tickets and account status, so we can act on them.

How we use it

  • To run your account, profile and reviews.
  • To keep one review per person per app and usernames unique.
  • To count views and downloads.
  • To handle feedback, reports and tickets, and to keep the store safe from spam, fake reviews and bad apps.
  • To send you service emails, such as a confirmation or reply to a report or ticket you sent.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. We may share information only when the law requires it, when an authority asks for it, or to protect our users and the store.

Paid placements

Some apps appear in lists marked Sponsored or Featured, and some developers have a Verified badge. Developers pay us for these. Paying never gives a developer access to your personal data.

Services we use

  • Firebase (Google) for sign-in (email or Google) and our database.
  • GitHub Pages to host the store's pages.
  • imgbb to host screenshots you attach to feedback or reports. Anyone with the exact link can open such an image, so do not include private details in a screenshot.
  • Google Apps Script and Gmail to send service emails.
  • Developer download links: when you tap Download you go to a link chosen by the app's developer, and their own privacy policy applies there.

These services may process basic technical data such as your IP address under their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Reports, tickets, feedback and account-status records may be kept for a reasonable time afterwards to stop repeat abuse and to settle disputes. Copies in backups or provider logs can remain for a short time after deletion.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and bio in Library, Settings, Edit profile. Your username is locked once chosen.
  • Delete your account in Library, Settings, Delete account. This removes your profile, username and all your reviews, and updates app ratings. Records described under How long we keep it may remain.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by email. Use any rights you have under the laws that apply to you.

Security

We use HTTPS and database security rules, and only our team can open reports, tickets and feedback contact details. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices.

Children

UptoZishu is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Continuing to use the store means you accept the update.
