Introduction

Welcome to Stardust Store ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Android application and related services.

By using Stardust Store, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the application.

Our Commitment We are committed to protecting your privacy. Stardust Store does not require an account, does not sell your data, and is designed to work with the minimum information necessary to deliver a smooth app-discovery and download experience.
2
Information We Collect

Stardust Store is built with privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the application.

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Profile Information — Display name, username, bio, and profile picture you set within the app.
  • Wishlist — Apps you have added to your personal wishlist.
  • Download History — Apps you have previously downloaded through the store.
  • Theme Preference — Whether you use Light or Dark mode.
  • Review Logs — A local record of reviews you have submitted.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the application.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you interact with the store, the following is collected:

Data Type Purpose
App Views Counts how many times an app's detail page is viewed to display popularity.
Download Count Tracks how many times an app is downloaded to rank apps in the store.
Reviews & Ratings Your submitted reviews and star ratings, published publicly with your chosen display name.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We Never Collect
  • Your contacts, SMS, call logs, or phone number
  • Your precise location (GPS) or background location
  • Your camera, microphone, or photo gallery contents
  • Your browsing history outside the app
  • Your device passwords, credentials, or private files
  • Any sensitive personal or financial information
3
How We Use Your Information

The limited information we collect is used solely to operate and improve Stardust Store:

  • Display apps and their details — to show app information, screenshots, and metadata.
  • Rank apps — to sort apps by popularity, downloads, and trending status.
  • Show your reviews — to display your reviews and ratings to other users.
  • Personalize your experience — to remember your theme preference, wishlist, and profile.
  • Enable downloads and installations — to fetch, download, and hand off APK files to Android's package installer.
  • Improve the service — to understand which apps and features are most used.
No Advertising Stardust Store does not display advertisements, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
4
Permissions Explained

Stardust Store requests the following Android permissions. Each permission is used only for its stated purpose:

INTERNET
Required to load app information from our servers and to download APK files securely over HTTPS.
REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES
Required to hand off downloaded APK files to Android's official package installer. Used only when you tap the Install button.
STORAGE (App-specific)
Used only to store downloaded APK files in the app's private external directory. No access to your personal files or media.
PACKAGE QUERY (System)
Used to detect whether an app is already installed on your device, to determine whether to show Install, Update, or Open.
You're In Control You can revoke any permission at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → Stardust Store → Permissions. Some features may not work without the required permissions.
5
Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

5.1 Local Storage

Your profile, wishlist, download history, and theme preference are stored only on your device using Android's local storage mechanisms. This data is never uploaded to our servers.

5.2 Network Security

All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. Download URLs are validated as HTTPS before any file is fetched. We reject non-secure (HTTP) connections.

5.3 APK Integrity

When available, we verify downloaded APK files against their published SHA-256 hashes to detect tampering or corruption.

5.4 No User Accounts

Stardust Store does not require account registration, passwords, or email addresses. There is nothing for us to protect because we simply do not collect it.

Secure by Design Stardust Store follows the principle of data minimization — we collect the least amount of data needed and store what we can locally on your device.
6
Third-Party Services

Stardust Store uses a small number of trusted third-party services to operate. Each service is described below:

6.1 Firebase Realtime Database

Provided by Google. Used to store the app catalog (app names, descriptions, screenshots, download URLs) and user-submitted reviews and ratings. Data stored in Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6.2 ImgBB

Used to host images that you optionally attach to app reviews. Images uploaded to ImgBB are subject to ImgBB's Privacy Policy. You are never required to attach images to reviews.

6.3 Android System Services

Downloads and APK installations are handled by Android's own system components. The Android Package Installer is invoked for all installations.

No Data Selling We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.
7
Children's Privacy

Stardust Store is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Stardust Store does not require account creation and stores personal data only on your own device, there is no mechanism for us to collect or store information about any user, including children.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the app (for example, via a review), please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Rights

You have full control over the information associated with your use of Stardust Store:

  • Access — All data stored on your device is directly visible to you within the app.
  • Deletion — You can clear all locally stored data at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → Stardust Store → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Wishlist Control — You can add or remove apps from your wishlist at any time.
  • Review Removal — You may request removal of your submitted reviews by contacting us.
  • Uninstall — Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
GDPR & CCPA If you are a resident of the European Union or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA. Because Stardust Store does not collect or transmit personal data to our servers, most of these rights are automatically satisfied. Contact us for any specific requests.
